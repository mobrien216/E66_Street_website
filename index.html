03 — Stage-Gate Process

Four disciplined stages

1
Research

Understand the problem, gather insights, generate potential solutions.

2
Incubate

Design experiments, identify assumptions to validate.

3
Pilot

Run small-scale experiments, collect feedback, refine.

4
Launch

Scale what's working, amplify the impact.

An idea can exit or wind down at any stage
04 — Scale & Exit

Where ideas land

Spin Off

Becomes its own nonprofit or B Corp.

Accelerate

Handed to a regional entrepreneurship accelerator partner to scale further.

Wind Down

The idea is retired; lessons are captured and carried forward.
