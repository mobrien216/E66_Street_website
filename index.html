Why It Matters

Every year, good ideas
never get their shot.

Civic innovation dies in the gap between a promising idea and its first dollar of funding. That gap is exactly where we work.

Without an early believer

  • Promising civic ideas stall before they ever reach a community
  • Grassroots innovators lack the capital and infrastructure to scale
  • Proven solutions stay siloed in one neighborhood instead of spreading citywide
  • Talented local problem-solvers leave for cities that will back them

With East 66th Street behind them

  • Early-stage civic ventures get the runway to prove their model
  • Founders gain mentorship, workspace, and a network of real partners
  • Successful pilots scale across Cleveland and beyond
  • Local talent stays, builds, and reinvests in its own community
We back the builders before anyone else will.

In Plain Terms

At East 66th Street Services, we know you want your giving to build something that lasts. That means investing in the earliest, riskiest stage of civic innovation — the stage most funders skip. The problem is that promising ideas rarely find funding before they can prove themselves, which makes real change feel like somebody else's job. We believe every neighborhood already has the people and ideas to solve its own hardest problems. We understand how frustrating it is to give and never see where it landed, which is why we incubate every venture we fund: space, mentorship, and a direct line to the partners who help it grow. So your investment doesn't just fund a program — it funds a pipeline of civic innovation for years to come. In the meantime, promising ideas keep dying on the vine for lack of an early believer. Your support means they don't have to.
